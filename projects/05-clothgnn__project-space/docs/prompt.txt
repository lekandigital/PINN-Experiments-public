MeshGraphNetRP Surrogate for Cloth & Hair

**Project title:** "ClothGNN"

**Mission:** Produce a turnkey research package for a MeshGraphNet-style recurrent surrogate that predicts cloth/hair vertex displacements under wind, collisions, and material tags.

**Deliver:**
1. Literature digest on MeshGraphNetRP, DiffCloth, Evo-Shear (SIGGRAPH 2022-24) with key takeaways.
2. Dataset builder: cloak 3s simulations from CLO3D/Marvelous Designer and Nvidia Flex, plus auto-labeled seams, triangle shear, strain energy density.
3. Collision module using voxelized occupancy grids fused with graph features.
4. PyTorch Geometric code: encoder→GRU→decoder, optional equivariant layers, and auxiliary losses on seam curvature & shear.
5. Training script with curriculum learning and data-augmentation knobs.
6. Export pipeline to USD and sample Unity/Omniverse integration snippets.
7. Evaluation notebook comparing FPS, Chamfer/Edge-length error vs. ground truth on RTX 4090 and laptop GPUs.

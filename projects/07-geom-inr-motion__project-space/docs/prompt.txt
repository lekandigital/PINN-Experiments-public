Geom-INR-Motion
Implicit Neural Representation for Human Motion

**Project title:** "Geom-INR-Motion"

**Mission:** Deliver a full research starter kit for an implicit neural representation with differential-geometry regularization for long-horizon human motion prediction and retargeting.

**Contents:**
1. Annotated survey of NeRMo, SkelINR, MotionDiff, and curvature-regularized motion works (2022-25).
2. Data pipeline converting AMASS/Human3.6M to continuous time-coordinate → joint-pose pairs; include skeleton normalizer.
3. PyTorch implementation: sine-activated INR network conditioned on actor ID, with optional graph-based kinematic constraint module.
4. Geometry losses penalizing torsion/curvature spikes; comparison to diffusion baselines.
5. Training script, LR scheduler, and checkpoint-to-Blender export (USD/FBX) producing in-between keyframes.
6. Evaluation notebook with FID-Motion, MPJPE, and perceptual smoothness metrics.
7. Plug-in template for Blender 4.x exposing a UI to load the trained weights and retarget any BVH file.

HGNN-ClothDyn: Graph-Based Dynamics
While NIFs excel at surface detail, they lack topological awareness. HGNN-ClothDyn treats the cloth mesh strictly as a graph.

We construct a Multi-Resolution Graph Pyramid based on geodesic adjacency, allowing the network to pass messages across long distances (drape behavior) and short distances (wrinkle behavior) simultaneously.

Instead of generic message passing, the graph layers utilize Learned Edge Stiffness parameters. The network effectively learns the spring constants of a mass-spring system, approximating Hookean constraints:

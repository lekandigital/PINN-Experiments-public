ClothGeom-NIF
Neural Implicit Field Decoder for High-Detail Cloth Geometry

**Project title:** "ClothGeom-NIF"

**Mission:** Create a geometry decoder that maps latent cloth states (node positions + edge strains) to watertight, high-detail cloth surfaces.

**Need:**
1. Survey of latent-conditioned SDF/NeRF decoders (DeepSDF+, INGP, EG3D-mesh).
2. Training set builder converting HGNN outputs to latent codes aligned with ground-truth SDFs.
3. PyTorch implementation: sine-activated MLP with latent modulation; variance field for LOD blending; real-time tricks (voxel hash cache, fp16).
4. Marching-cubes export pipeline with progressive resolution control.
5. Inverse-design notebook: optimise latent to hit target silhouette or drape.
6. Evaluation metrics (Chamfer, normal consistency) vs. ground truth at multiple LODs.
7. Deployment demo in Unity/Omniverse with live latent sliders.

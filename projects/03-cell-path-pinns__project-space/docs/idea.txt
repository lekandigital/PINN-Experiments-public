Cell-Path PINNs
In biological modeling, we forecast microbe chemotaxis (movement towards nutrients) by treating the nutrient concentration as a Latent Potential Field.

The neural network learns the manifold of this potential field, and the loss function enforces a Geodesic Constraint: microbes are modeled as minimizing energy paths (geodesics) on this evolving manifold. This provides a geometric framework for understanding biological efficiency.
